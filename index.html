Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you buy.

How do I get support?

Open a ticket in the Discord server. Support, license keys and bug reports are handled there.

How do I receive my product?

After payment is confirmed, you receive download access and a unique license key inside your ticket.

What payment methods are accepted?

Payments are made through a secure payment link provided inside your Discord ticket.

Are the macros safe to use?

Automation may not be allowed in every game. Always check the game’s rules before using any macro, and use tools at your own risk.

Can I share my license key?

No. License keys are personal. Shared or resold keys may be revoked without a refund.

What do I need to run the macros?

A Windows PC. Standalone .exe versions do not require extra software.
